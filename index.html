About Me

I currently work as an IT Officer at GB Microfinance Institution Plc., where I support IT infrastructure, network systems, and Core Banking operations, while completing my final year of a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Western University. Balancing a full-time technical role with my studies has given me a practical, systems-first view of software: I don't just write code, I understand the infrastructure it runs on and the users who depend on it.
